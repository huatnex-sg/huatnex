# HuatNex MVP

An early-preview web page that helps buyers judge whether a Singapore new-launch condo or EC is likely to grow in value by the time it can be sold without Seller's Stamp Duty.

Live site: https://huatnex-sg.github.io/huatnex/

## Data sources
- Urban Redevelopment Authority (URA): Master Plan 2025 and 2019 layers, Government Land Sales, private residential transactions, private residential price index. Contains information from URA.
- Housing & Development Board (HDB): resale flat prices, property information, EC sites sold.
- Ministry of Education (MOE): general information of schools.
- Singapore Land Authority (SLA): OneMap.
- Datasets from data.gov.sg are used under the Singapore Open Data Licence (https://data.gov.sg/open-data-licence).

## Disclaimer
This is not financial advice or a valuation. Forecasts are estimates from past price trends and may be wrong.
